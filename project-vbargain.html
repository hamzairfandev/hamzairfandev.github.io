V bargain plugin

V Bargain is an innovative WooCommerce plugin designed to transform the way customers shop online. This powerful tool introduces a unique bargaining feature that allows customers to negotiate product prices directly from the single product page. With its user-friendly interface, Bargain Buddy simplifies price negotiations, making the shopping experience interactive and engaging.

The plugin enables customers to submit their price requests through a simple form. These requests are then processed and displayed in a custom menu within the WordPress admin panel, where store owners can review, accept, or counter the offers. The plugin also provides customers with a dedicated section in their “My Account” page to track the status of their requests and respond to admin counteroffers.

Store owners have full control over this feature. On the product edit page, they can enable or disable the bargaining option for specific products and set up price ranges for negotiation. Once a bargain is accepted, the product becomes available for purchase at the negotiated price, ensuring a seamless transaction.

It has an additional feature where the admin can set prices on the product edit page in the backend, and customers can also add their own prices. If the customer's price is greater than or equal to the defined price, the product will be automatically added to the cart at that price.

V Bargain empowers businesses to enhance customer engagement, boost conversions, and create a personalized shopping experience. It's a must-have solution for stores looking to stand out in a competitive eCommerce landscape.
